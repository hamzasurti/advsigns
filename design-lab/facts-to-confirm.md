- **Certified payroll on prevailing-wage jobs.** The first version of the site said this. See row 7.
- **Your 26 install cities.** From your own list.
- **Customer reviews.** They read as real reviews (named people, real details, some from Yelp). Kept unchanged.
- **The ADA rules on the ADA page.** These come from the 2010 ADA Standards, section 703, not from us. We checked them. Characters are raised at least 1/32 in., uppercase and sans serif, 5/8 in. to 2 in. high. Braille is Grade 2 with domed or rounded dots, below the text. Mounting height is 48 to 60 in. to the baseline, on the latch side of the door. We corrected one line: braille sits below the text and at least 3/8 in. from it, not "directly below". The note now says California's Building Code (Title 24, Chapter 11B) also applies.
